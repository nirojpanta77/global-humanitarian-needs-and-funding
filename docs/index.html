Methodology & sources

Source: OCHA HPC public API, with plan-linked financial fields from the annual plan summary. The financial-source selector also allows comparison with the independently retrieved, plan-ID-scoped FTS report. FTS shared funding is not allocated to individual sectors. Data updates follow the repository's scheduled refresh, not real-time transactions.

Interpretation and limitations

Default plan scope includes HRP/HNRP (excluding named addenda) and explicit replacement appeals for Ukraine 2022 and Palestine 2024–2026. Strict HRP/HNRP excludes these exceptions. Other appeals remain accessible under All response plans. If regional plans are wanted, select All response plans and Include regional plans. Plan-level people in need and targets can overlap across plans. Sector-level populations must not be summed across sectors. Overall Protection is separate from its constituent areas; choose one sector at a time. Funding coverage uses only records with both a reported requirement and funding value. COVID-specific plans and caseloads are excluded from the analytical layer, but mixed-plan financial totals may still include COVID funding. Multi-year plans may repeat plan-lifecycle funding; annual totals are planning-year views, not annual spending. Missing amounts are not zero. Historical changes reflect both needs and changes in methodology, plan scope and reporting.
